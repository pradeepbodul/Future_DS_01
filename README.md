📊 Business Sales Performance Analysis

📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing historical business sales data to evaluate overall performance, profitability, product trends, and regional effectiveness. The goal is to deliver a client-ready Power BI dashboard along with a structured analytical report that provides actionable business insights.

The project follows a complete end-to-end data analytics workflow, similar to real-world analytics tasks performed for businesses, startups, and consulting clients.

🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze sales and profit performance over time
* Identify top-performing and underperforming product categories and sub-categories
* Evaluate regional and regional manager performance
* Measure the impact of returned orders on profitability
* Generate clear, data-driven business insights and recommendations

🗂️ Dataset Description

The analysis is based on a structured sales dataset consisting of three tables:

1️⃣ Orders

* Transaction-level sales data
* Key fields: Order ID, Order Date, Ship Date, Customer Details, Category, Sub-Category, Sales, Quantity, Discount, Profit
* Time period: 2021–2024

2️⃣ People

* Maps Regions to Regional Managers
* Used for manager-wise performance analysis

3️⃣ Returns

* Contains returned order records
* Used to calculate return rate and analyze return impact

🧹 Data Cleaning & Preprocessing

Data preparation was performed using Power BI Power Query to ensure clean, consistent, and analysis-ready data.

Key Steps:

* Removed non-business columns (e.g., Row ID)
* Verified and corrected data types for date and numeric fields
* Removed duplicate records from the Returns table
* Renamed columns for clarity and consistency
* Created time-based features:
  * Year
  * Month
  * Month Name
  * Quarter
  * Year–Month
* Derived business metrics:
  * Profit Margin
  * Profit Status (Loss, Medium Profit, High Profit)

📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The following KPIs were defined to measure business performance:
Total Sales
Total Profit
Profit Margin (%)
Total Orders
Total Quantity Sold
Average Order Value (AOV)
Return Rate (%)

🔍 Analysis & Key Insights

📊 Overall Performance

* Total Sales: 2.33M
* Total Profit: 292.30K
* Profit Margin: 12.56%
* Average Order Value: 455.20
* Return Rate: 5.79%

The business shows strong revenue generation with healthy profitability and a controlled return rate.

📈 Sales & Profit Trends

* Sales and profit display a steady upward trend over time
* Performance in 2023 and 2024 is significantly stronger than earlier years
* Growth is consistent with minimal volatility

🛍️ Product Category Analysis

* Technology is the highest-performing category in both sales and profit
* Office Supplies shows stable sales with good profitability
* Furniture has relatively high sales but very low profit margins

> High sales do not always translate into high profitability.

🌍 Regional & Manager Analysis

* West and East regions contribute the highest sales and profit
* Central and South regions show lower performance
* Manager-wise analysis highlights clear performance differences across regions

🔄 Returns Analysis

* Returned Orders: 296
* Return Rate: 5.79%

Returns are within acceptable limits but still present opportunities to improve margins.

💡 Business Recommendations

1. Focus more on high-margin categories, especially Technology
2. Improve Furniture category profitability by reviewing pricing and discounts
3. Replicate successful sales strategies from West and East regions in underperforming regions
4. Promote high-profit products rather than only high-volume products
5. Reduce returns by improving product quality checks and customer expectations
